About

I'm Sayed Shaheen Alam. For my day job, I manage a regional sales territory — setting targets, coaching field officers, and reading the numbers that decide whether a quarter is good or bad.

On the side, I taught myself Python, SQL, and full-stack development to solve a problem I kept running into as a manager: the tools I was given never showed me what I actually needed to see. So I started building my own — a Sales ERP that reports the way a sales manager actually thinks, with an AI layer that writes the executive summary for me.

The long-term goal is simple: turn that domain knowledge and this codebase into real software for Bangladeshi SMEs who are still running their sales on spreadsheets.
